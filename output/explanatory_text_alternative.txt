The chart created is a line plot, illustrating the number of follow-up visits for each program type, and comparing it to the exercise goals met by participants. The x-axis represents the number of sessions completed, while the y-axis is the percentage of participants meeting the exercise goal. The Home-based program showed an increase from 58% at visit 1 to 70% at visit 4, while the center-based program was 58% at visit 1 to 79% at visit 4, which is a 9% difference. But because it was the participant's choice which program they wanted to participate in, this does not necessarily mean the center-based program causes participants to meet their exercise goals more. The difference could also not be accounting for those who chose to drop out from the programs.
